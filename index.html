About this policy

This policy covers the ThugeurBP landing page and provides a general framework for ThugeurBP games. Information handling in a particular game depends on the features and services it actually uses. Review that game’s store disclosures, in-game notices, and any game-specific privacy policy for the applicable details.

01. Who we are and what this covers

ThugeurBP is the studio identified on this website. For questions about this policy or information you share with us, contact duongthianhbp2024@gmail.com.

This policy explains information handling when you visit this website, contact the studio, or use a ThugeurBP game that links to this policy. A game-specific notice supplements this policy and takes precedence for that game where the notices differ.

02. Information on this website

This landing page has no account registration, contact form, analytics, advertising SDK, tracking pixel, cookies, or browser storage. Its artwork, styles, and scripts are embedded in the HTML; the page does not load third-party fonts or images.

When hosted online, the hosting provider may process technical request data, such as an IP address, browser information, timestamps, and access logs, to deliver and secure the website. The host’s own terms and privacy notice govern its processing.

Information you choose to send

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message, attachments, and any other information you provide. For game support, this may include a game name, player identifier, device model, screenshots, or purchase reference. Send only information relevant to your request; do not send passwords or full payment-card details.

Information in games, where applicable

A particular game may process the following categories only when the relevant functionality is present. The game’s disclosures should identify which categories actually apply.

  • Gameplay information: progress, settings, achievements, session activity, and a game-generated player identifier.
  • Technical and diagnostic information: device model, operating system, app version, language, error reports, and performance information.
  • Advertising information: advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and approximate region inferred from an IP address, if the game uses advertising services and applicable settings permit it.
  • Purchase information: product identifiers, purchase status, transaction references, and entitlement information if in-app purchases are offered.
  • Account or social information: information you authorize a sign-in platform to share, if optional account or social features are provided.

Gameplay and settings may remain on your device where a game uses local saves. Device permissions, such as access to photos or a microphone, should be requested only when needed for a clearly described feature. This website does not request those permissions.

03. Why information is used

Depending on the service, information may be used to:

  • Respond to support requests, questions, and other messages.
  • Provide gameplay, save progress, restore purchases, and operate requested features.
  • Identify crashes, resolve technical problems, and improve stability and usability.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access and protect service integrity.
  • Deliver and measure advertisements, if the game includes advertising.
  • Meet applicable obligations and handle disputes or lawful requests.

Legal grounds, where required

Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis, processing must rely on an appropriate ground: providing a requested service, consent, a legal obligation, or legitimate interests balanced against your rights. Consent-dependent processing should occur only after the required consent. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it using the available settings or by contacting us; withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing.

04. Service providers and disclosures

Where necessary for a service, information may be handled by providers that support hosting, email, crash reporting, analytics, cloud saves, advertising, or payment verification. The providers used by a particular game should be identified in its game-specific notice or store disclosures; this policy does not assert that any particular SDK is installed.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to respond to valid legal process, or where necessary to protect people, rights, and service security. If a business transfer affects information, applicable privacy obligations continue to apply and any required notice will be provided.

Advertising and analytics, if included in a game

An ad-supported game may display contextual or personalized ads. Personalization, measurement, and access to advertising identifiers depend on the services used, your region, permissions, consent, and device settings. Analytics, if used, may help explain gameplay activity and technical performance. This landing page includes neither advertising nor analytics.

You can use available in-game privacy controls and your device’s advertising or tracking settings to manage these choices. Restricting personalization may still leave contextual advertising visible. If a game’s data practices fall within a jurisdiction’s definition of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising, the required notice and opt-out mechanisms must be provided for that game.

Purchases and external services

If a game offers purchases, the relevant app store or payment provider handles checkout under its own terms and privacy policy. Support requests should include only a transaction reference or receipt with unnecessary personal details removed. External sites, stores, and services operate under their own privacy notices.

05. Children’s privacy

This website is a general studio information page and does not provide accounts or request children’s information. A game’s intended audience and age rating should be checked in its store listing and game-specific notice.

For a game directed to children, or where a user is known to be below the applicable age of consent, applicable child-privacy requirements must be followed, including limits on collection and advertising and parental consent where required. A store age rating alone does not establish consent to data processing.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to the studio without the required authorization, contact us. Identify the relevant game or message so the concern can be investigated and appropriate action taken, including deletion where required.

06. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of certain advertising-related uses. Some requests may be limited by legal requirements or information necessary to resolve an ongoing issue.

To make a request, email duongthianhbp2024@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request.” State the relevant game or service, your request, and an identifier that helps locate the information. Do not send identity documents unless a necessary, proportionate verification process has been explained.

Requests will be handled within the timeframe required by applicable law. We may need to verify that the request relates to your information. Where provided by law, you may use an authorized agent, appeal a decision, or complain to your local data-protection authority. Exercising privacy rights should not result in unlawful discrimination.

Device data and permissions

You can manage permissions in your device settings. For a game that stores progress only locally, clearing app data or uninstalling may remove those saves. Uninstalling does not automatically delete information already held by the studio or a third-party provider; submit a request to the relevant party where needed.

07. Retention, security, and transfers

How long information is kept

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose described, including responding to support, providing requested functionality, preventing abuse, and satisfying applicable requirements. The relevant period depends on the category, purpose, and service. Deletion requests are subject to lawful retention exceptions; backup copies may persist until their normal deletion cycle.

Protecting information

Safeguards should be appropriate to the nature of the information and the service. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. If you suspect a privacy or security issue, contact us without including passwords or other secrets.

International processing

Email, hosting, and game-service providers may operate in countries other than your own. Where a service involves an international transfer subject to legal restrictions, an appropriate transfer mechanism and safeguards must be used as required by applicable law. Contact us for questions about a specific service.

08. Policy updates and contact

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in services or information handling. The effective and updated dates appear at the top. Material changes should be communicated through the website or the affected game, and fresh consent obtained where required.
